   Trust scores now derived from measurement, not dice.

## The unifying insight

The stack is not built from math. It is built from cuts — binary
distinctions held open by invariants. The golden ratio is not chosen; it
emerges because every layer is recursive: heat is variance of variance's
structure, cascade eigenvalues are phi^-n, soft-contract contracts toward a
center no iteration attains. Any architecture whose parts must resemble the
whole, whose rules must not decompose (deny-overrides-allow cannot split),
converges on x = 1 + 1/x. Five is where decomposition stops (A5 simple).
The cone is where a cut touches zero heat. OBSERVE has no parent because
you cannot watch the watcher. HOLD_UNLINEAGED is the first state that is
not a measurement failure but a category error: absence of origin.
